# Contributing

Contributions to the aesthetics-clinic landing-page skill are welcome.

- Keep facts, hypotheses, recommendations, and pending items distinct.
- Do not add patient records, identifiable clinical data, credentials, private URLs, or real client examples without explicit authorization.
- Verify profession-specific guidance with current official primary sources and cite it in the relevant reference.
- Preserve the skill’s four-stage workflow and its boundary between planning and implementation/publication.
- Run python3 scripts/validate_skills.py and the packaged validate_delivery.py when changing their respective files.
- Submit focused changes with a clear explanation of the user-facing effect.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the repository’s MIT License.
